Welcome to the Annotation Tool!

1

Understanding the Interface

The annotation tool has two main panels:
  • Left Panel: Video playback controls and the annotation frame where boxes are displayed
  • Right Panel: Annotation controls and instructions
2

Review the Bounding Boxes

Pre-drawn boxes around people will be displayed on the annotation frame. Each box represents a detected person and is labeled as "Person 1", "Person 2", etc.
💡 Tip: You can watch the video preview to better understand who is interacting with whom.
3

Answer Pairwise Questions

For each pair of people, you'll be asked: "Are these two people in the same group?"
  • Click "Yes" (or press Y) if they appear to be together
  • Click "No" (or press N) if they appear to be in different groups
  • Click "Unsure" (or press U) if you are not sure whether they form a group.
  • Click "Incomplete Group" (or press I) if a group appears to be missing a member.
Continue until all pairs are annotated.
4

Video Controls

Use the video controls to help make your decisions:
  • Play/Pause: Shift key
  • Next Frame: Right arrow (→)
  • Previous Frame: Left arrow (←)
  • Skip frames: Shift + Arrow keys
  • Scrubber: Drag to jump to any frame
5

Submit Your Annotation

Once you've answered all pairwise questions, the "Submit Annotation" button will be enabled. Click it to save your work and move to the next video.
✅ All your annotations are automatically saved to the database.

📝 Annotation Tool

📋 How to Annotate

  1. Review the boxes shown on the annotation image
  2. For each highlighted set of people (one or more individuals), decide if they are in the same group.
  3. Click "Yes" if they form one complete group, "No" if they're separate, "Unsure" if unclear, or "Incomplete Group" if a member is missing.
  4. Use keyboard shortcuts: Y (Yes), N (No), U (Unsure), I (Incomplete).
  5. Continue until all pairs are annotated
  6. Click "Submit Annotation" when done
💡 Tip: Watch the video preview to better understand group dynamics and relationships.
