📝 Instructions

📋 How to Use

  1. Auto-Detection: Person detection runs automatically when each video loads. Detected persons appear as colored boxes on the annotation frame. Each group gets a unique color for easy distinction.
  2. Select & Highlight:
    • Click a box on the canvas to select it — the box will highlight and the corresponding detection will be highlighted in the list.
    • Click an item in the Detections list to highlight the corresponding box on the canvas.
  3. Edit Labels: Double-click a box on the canvas or click the "Edit" button next to a detection in the list. You can:
    • Change the person's group assignment.
    • Convert "individual" persons to a new group.
    • Change to "individual" if needed.
  4. Delete Detections: Click the "×" button next to a detection to remove it. Groups will automatically renumber sequentially.
  5. Watch Video: The video preview (left-bottom) shows all 50 frames to help you understand the scene context. Use play/pause and the frame scrubber to navigate.
  6. Submit: When satisfied, click Submit. It saves the current video and moves to the next one automatically. On the last video it saves everything and downloads the full annotation JSON.
